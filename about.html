About Us

PROJECT

The Aim of the Project is to contribute to the development of Multipurpose sports and delivering the social, health and community benefits that are well-recognised with community participation in and support for sporting teams and competitions.

PROJECT OBJECTIVES

The overall goal of the project is to promote through participation in sports activity while ensuring many health and social benefits inherent in sporting activity form greater outcomes beyond sporting arena into everyday life.
Provide appropriate infrastructure in order to Multi Sports, recreation and physical activity to promote health agenda.
Provide small students Fun Games and Physical Activities.

BENEFITS

Participants in Multisports training may benefit from improved speed and agility, increase strength and endurance, and better injury prevention effort. Each Sports will also enable participants to learn about the impact of healthy mind and body.
